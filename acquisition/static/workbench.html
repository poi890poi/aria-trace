Acquisition Workbench

Capture an uninterrupted human take: every frame, control state, control duration, camera behavior, and timestamp. Live replay will adapt these behaviors from visual feedback rather than replaying them on a fixed clock.

3

Perform uninterrupted takes

No in-game recorder commands. Queue a take, switch to the game, and play naturally. Frame and input sources pre-arm immediately; the take clock starts on the first selected-game focus and ends automatically after the configured duration. Losing focus early invalidates the take.

Visual landmarks are reference observations derived or corrected after recording. You never mark them while playing.

4

Confirm, compile, and evaluate

After gameplay, confirm each full-take boundary or use the reviewer to tighten it. Run 1 becomes the reference; later takes remain held out.
